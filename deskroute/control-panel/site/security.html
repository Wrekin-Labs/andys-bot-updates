security

Clear boundaries for customer support.

Access control, private collaboration and reviewed knowledge are built into the workflow.

01

Workspace access

Staff sign in to their workspace. Database checks scope protected workflows to the current user’s membership and permissions.

02

Private by default

Internal notes are excluded from the website chat delivery path. Help Centre drafts and disabled centres stay outside public publication.

03

Traceable actions

Reply and internal-note workflows record audit events. The interface also exposes recent workspace audit activity.

04

Before a commercial agreement

Confirm the operator’s privacy notice, data-processing terms, retention policy and support contact during onboarding. No certification or compliance guarantee is claimed on this preview.
