Privacy overview

How DeskRoute is designed to handle customer-support data during the commercial preview.

This page is a product/privacy overview for the commercial preview, not a substitute for the final operator privacy notice.

What DeskRoute processes

DeskRoute can process support conversations, contact details supplied by visitors, business knowledge, staff account data, notification/device records and operational audit events.

Why

The data is used to deliver the support workspace, website chat, approved-knowledge answers, human handoff and operational security.

Commercial sign-off

Before live paid onboarding, the operator must publish the final privacy notice, retention periods, controller/processor roles and rights-request contact.
