help centre

Useful answers, on your customers’ terms.

Publish a simple, searchable Help Centre from reviewed articles.

01

Write privately

A new centre begins private. Create plain-text article drafts and organise them by category.

02

Publish intentionally

An article is public only when it is published and its centre is enabled. Editing an article returns it to draft.

03

Make answers easy to find

Customers can search article titles and content from the public centre link. They do not need a staff account.

04

Keep publication separate

Approving a Business Brain fact does not automatically publish a Help Centre article. Review both for their audience.
