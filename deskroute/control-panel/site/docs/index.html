Sign in and workspace access

Open your DeskRoute workspace link and sign in with the account supplied during onboarding. Choose the correct business in the workspace selector. On mobile, the same selector is in Settings.

If you cannot sign in, check the email address and contact your workspace owner through your established channel. Choose Set or reset your password on the sign-in page to request a recovery email for an existing account. Open the newest email link and choose your own password; use that same account on desktop and phone. New workspace access must be arranged by an owner. Never share your password with support.

Owners and admins manage workspace settings. Agents reply and add notes. Viewers read. Custom roles use their configured permissions.

Your first successful handoff

  1. Confirm who owns the workspace and who will watch the Human Queue.
  2. Add a small set of verified business facts: services, hours, contact, pricing and policies.
  3. Review each fact in Business Brain. Keep uncertain information pending.
  4. Install the website widget on a staging page and configure its allowed origin.
  5. Ask a question covered by approved knowledge. Confirm the reply.
  6. Ask a question outside that knowledge. Confirm the gap, assignment and staff alert.
  7. Add an internal note and confirm it never appears in the visitor chat.
  8. Send a public reply, reconnect the visitor chat and confirm it appears once.

Keep automatic mode off until these checks pass for your business.

Build and maintain the Business Brain

Choose Business Brain → Add fact, or Scan website. Scan only a website you are authorised to manage. A scan proposes facts for review; it does not install the widget or approve its own answers.

Expand a fact to inspect the answer and source. Approve only information you have checked. Editing an approved fact returns it to pending. Do not put customer personal data or secrets in general business knowledge.

Knowledge Gaps show questions that could not be answered safely. Teach an answer, review it, and approve it. Learned FAQ suggestions also require approval.

Settings provides Suggest only, Review before sending and Automatic approved answers. Automatic answers require approved facts and at least 90% confidence. Confidence and readiness scores are indicators, not guarantees.

Reply, collaborate and resolve

Open Inbox or Human Queue and select a conversation. The header provides status, priority and assignee. A public website reply becomes available to the visitor’s chat on its next successful sync; “saved” is not proof that the visitor has read it.

Choose Internal Note for private team discussion. You can mention another member of the same workspace. Notes never belong in the public reply box.

Mark pending when you are waiting on something. Resolve when the conversation is complete. Reopen when work resumes. Changing status does not send a reply.

Text drafts remain in memory while switching conversations. They are cleared on sign-out or workspace change and are not an offline outbox.

The inbox shows the most recent 200 conversations and up to 200 recent messages per open thread. Search applies to the loaded conversations.

Install website chat and check channels

Open Channels → Website Chat → Installation & widgets. Copy the snippet for the correct brand. Install it once before the closing body tag or in your website’s approved script area.

<script src="https://deskroute-widget-v6-0-1.onrender.com/deskroute-widget.js"
  data-deskroute-key="YOUR_WIDGET_PUBLIC_KEY" defer></script>

The public widget key is an installation identifier, not a staff credential. Never put a secret or service-role key in a website. Use the exact allowed origins shown for the widget, including the correct www/non-www hostname.

Email, WhatsApp, Telegram and X require provider authorisation and separate inbound/outbound delivery checks. The 6.1 reply composer only sends website chat replies. Add an internal note or use your established provider workflow for other channels.

Review routing rules

Open Automations and create a rule for an incoming message. Select a channel or message keyword, then a priority, assignee or tag. Set the order and choose whether matching stops further rules.

Saving creates or updates a disabled rule. Review the conditions and test the outcome before enabling it. Time-based rules require a separately verified scheduler; do not assume they run because they appear in the list.

Publish a Help Centre

Create a help centre for your brand. It starts private. Add an article with a title, URL slug, category and plain-text body. Review the draft, publish the article and then enable the centre.

Use Open public centre to check what an anonymous visitor sees. Only published articles in an enabled centre are returned. Unpublish an article or make the centre private to remove access. Saving an article edit returns it to draft.

Help articles and approved AI facts are separate publication decisions. Keep both up to date.

Alerts, mobile and desktop

Install the app from a supported browser’s menu. On iPhone or iPad, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android and desktop, use Install app when offered. This is a web app; a separate app-store package is not included.

Notifications include assignments and internal mentions. In Settings, select the types of alerts you want. Open an alert to visit the conversation or mark it read.

Keep DeskRoute open to receive in-app updates. Alerts refresh while the app is visible and online. Closed-app push is not enabled. Device last-seen activity does not prove that a teammate is available.

Understand the numbers

Analytics shows the selected brand’s last 30 days and a seven-day volume chart. It includes conversations, human replies, resolved work, pending drafts and knowledge gaps. Check the period and the chart’s 1,000-conversation limit before comparing figures.

Knowledge readiness measures topic coverage and unresolved gaps. A high score does not replace checking the business’s current facts.

Troubleshoot safely

  • Page failed to load: check your connection, retry, and sign in again if the session ended.
  • A save was rejected: confirm the correct workspace and your role, then refresh. A permission error is not a successful save.
  • Widget missing: check the snippet is installed once, the key matches, the widget is enabled and the origin is allowed.
  • Reply not visible: confirm it was a Public Reply on Website Chat, reconnect the visitor chat and check the same conversation.
  • No alerts: keep the app open, check the connection and notification preferences. Background push is not available.
  • Offline: the shell may load, but data and saves need a connection. Do not assume unsent text has been queued.

Send your onboarding contact the page, time, browser and steps, with a redacted screenshot. Do not send tokens or passwords.

6.1 release boundaries

This is a release candidate. Website chat is the supported reply path. Paid self-service checkout, closed-app push, additional provider delivery and scheduled automations are not advertised as verified.

Commercial onboarding must confirm the support contact, support hours, plan limits, fees, privacy/data-processing terms and retention policy. The preview takes no payment.
