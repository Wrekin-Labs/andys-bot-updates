# Pricing hypothesis — not final

Published entry prices in this category run from roughly £39/month in UK-focused products to $99/store/month for larger repair/POS suites.

BenchRoute should test a simple ladder rather than compete only on price.

## Solo — £29/month
- 1 staff login
- 1 location
- unlimited repairs
- customers
- quotes/invoices
- website enquiries
- customer tracker
- email updates
- basic stock
- technical measurements and repair evidence

## Workshop — £59/month
- up to 5 staff
- 1 location
- everything in Solo
- staff assignments/tasks
- advanced stock and purchase orders
- configurable workflows
- analytics
- AI workshop assistant allowance
- priority email support

## Multi-site — £99/month
- up to 10 staff
- 2 locations included
- cross-location reporting
- advanced permissions
- custom branding/domain options
- higher automation allowance
- priority support

Extra locations and high-volume messaging can be usage-priced later.

## Trial
14 days, no card required.

## Validation questions
- Will a one-person electronics shop pay £29 to replace paper/spreadsheets?
- Is £59 clearly simpler than moving to a POS-heavy suite?
- How much support time does onboarding consume?
- What is the true per-shop cost of email, storage, AI and backups?
- Should AI be bundled or an add-on?

Pricing remains a hypothesis until five external shops complete real-workflow trials.
